Real face or AI-generated?

We are studying how students tell photographs of real people from faces created by a neural network, and how viewing time affects this.

  • You will be shown a few dozen faces, one at a time. For each one, choose “Real photo” or “AI-generated”.
  • It takes about 5–7 minutes. In half of the trials the face is shown for at most 3 seconds; in the others for as long as you need.
  • Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page: incomplete data is not saved.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail or other personal data. Only your answers, response times, field of study (a general group), device type and whether you had seen the test before are saved.
  • At the end you can see which of your answers were right and which were wrong.

Please take the test only once. A second attempt would not be comparable with the others and cannot be used.
